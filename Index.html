Critical Elector Education

4 Things to Know Before Casting Your Ballot

1

Town vs. Regional Jurisdiction Over Housing

The Town of Ajax cannot independently expropriate land for public housing or impose rent control (which is governed provincially). In Durham Region, subsidized public housing, emergency shelters, and Durham Region Transit (DRT) are managed by the Regional Council. This is why the Ward 3 Regional Councillor race carries far greater weight for socialist housing priorities than the Local Councillor race.

2

"Under-Voting" is Your Democratic Right

In Ontario municipal elections, you are never required to vote for all four offices. If you only feel confident about your Councillor picks, you can leave the Mayor or School Trustee races blank or click "Abstain". In Canadian voting, an under-vote will never spoil your ballot; your selected votes are counted with 100% validity.

3

The Public School Trustee "Vote for Two" Rule

Unlike municipal council seats where you select only one candidate, Ajax elects two (2) trustees to the Durham District School Board (DDSB) and Durham Catholic Board (DCDSB). You can vote for two candidates on the same ballot. The progressive labour slate features Michelle Macnamara AND Zubair Hussain to maximize public education defense.

4

The Hidden Trap of "Tax & Staffing Freezes"

Many municipal candidates campaign on "holding the line on taxes" or "freezing municipal staff". From an anti-austerity perspective, persistent property tax freezes and staff freezes deplete public transit frequency, reduce library and recreation operating hours, and force municipalities to privatize public operations. Adequate public tax revenue is essential to sustain public infrastructure.
